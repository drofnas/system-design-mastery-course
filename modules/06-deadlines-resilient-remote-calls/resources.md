## Accessibility and fallback

RES-07 offers video, audio, and slides. Learners unable to use the recording use
the slides with Lesson 8 for the same assignment. Every resource has a complete
local text alternative named in `module.json`; no account or payment is needed.

gRPC documentation, Google SRE material, Amazon practitioner writing, and

source text into learner artifacts; cite the relevant section and paraphrase.
