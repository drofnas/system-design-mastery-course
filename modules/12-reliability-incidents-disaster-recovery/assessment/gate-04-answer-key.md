# Gate 4 Assessor Notes and Explained Boundaries

> **PESD V1 historical contract:** Preserve this file for V1 learners and immutable evidence. PESD 2.0 uses [gates/G04/README.md](../../../gates/G04/README.md).

Use only after all four parts are frozen. These notes describe acceptable
reasoning, not one architecture or capstone answer.

## Written examination

1. Credit explicit valid/good events, missing telemetry, budget count, burn
   formula, and decisions. Dependency timeouts after acceptance stay visible.
2. Credit graphs distinguishing required serial, optional, fallback, parallel,
   and common-cause paths. Independence must be justified.
3. Credit long/short-window material active burn, reset behavior, low-traffic
   handling, priority degradation, and component telemetry used diagnostically.
4. Credit durable workflow history, stable effect identity, higher epoch,
   stale-owner rejection, replay boundaries, and authority reconciliation.
5. Credit measured versions/timestamps, isolated restore, minimum-service RTO,
   capacity and shed work, staged failback, destructive-target checks, audit,
   and rollback.

## Hidden practical

Correct fault names after reveal cannot repair changed predictions or mismatched
work. Pass evidence keeps consensus authority, workflow/effect identity,
journey measurement, recovery versions, hashes, and invariant oracles consistent
while acknowledging the model boundary.

## Defense and portfolio

Accept designs different from Northstar when user/data consequence, evidence,
security, cost, staffing, ownership, migration, rollback, dissent, and reversal
align. Gate 4 and the Week 48 revision must be new artifacts; earlier evidence
remains untouched.
