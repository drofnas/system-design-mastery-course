# Module 12 Semantic Readiness Review

## Curriculum alignment

The package covers every Module 12 syllabus question and required topic. It
connects user-journey measurement to error budgets, multi-window burn alerts,
bounded degradation, incident command, evidence-based postmortems, backup and
restore integrity, data-loss recovery, regional failover, fencing,
reconciliation, safe failback, and Gate 4.

## Teaching sufficiency

Eight local lessons teach mechanisms and repeatable decision procedures before
EX-01–EX-16 and the independent worksheets require them. Every lesson contains
outcomes, prerequisites, explanation, derivation or procedure, a worked
Northstar example, expert mistakes, guided practice, self-checks, explained
answers, and authoritative citations. Required sources reinforce rather than
replace local teaching.

## Case and capstone isolation

Northstar continues the observatory case with public catalog, publication,
regional serving, and authoritative observation data. It introduces no commerce
entities or canonical capstone topology. Teaching, answer keys, fixtures, and
Gate 4 assessor notes require the learner to freeze independent commerce work
first. The Week 48 revision is a protected new artifact; no earlier baseline or
revision is modified.

## Interface and evidence integrity

The public scenario and trial schemas constrain exact top-level contracts.
Eighteen deterministic scenarios form nine same-input broken/repaired pairs;
each pair changes one named control, preserves shared-input hashes, exposes its
declared broken invariant, and restores I01–I12 when repaired. Scenario,
shared-input, and configuration hashes preserve the comparison boundary.

## Operational judgment

Instruction and assessment cover reliability, security and break-glass access,
capacity, cost tiers, ownership, staffing, migration, rollout, rollback,
dissent, communication, audit, and reversal evidence. The rubric does not
reward reliability vocabulary without a causal model or measured recovery.

## Assessment integrity

G01–G06 run before R01–R10. R04, R07, R08, and R09 are safety-critical.
Evaluators must cite files and headings, classify findings, accept defensible
alternatives, and recommend lessons and exercises without writing replacement
graded work. Pass, Revise, and Repeat fixtures were each evaluated twice; bands
and every criterion agree exactly.

## Evidence boundary

The deterministic model cannot prove production availability, physical backup
durability, provider control-plane independence, real regional isolation,
human performance under stress, security enforcement, or regulatory
compliance. The module states those limits wherever model evidence is used.

## Conclusion

Semantic review passes. No missing teaching dependency, capstone answer
leakage, learner-evidence overwrite, unsupported production proof, unsafe
operator instruction, or Phase III scope gap was found.
