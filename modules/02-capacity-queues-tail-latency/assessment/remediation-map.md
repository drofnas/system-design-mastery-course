# Module 2 Remediation Map

Remediation adds evidence beside the frozen prediction and raw trials; it never
normalizes or replaces them.

| Finding | Revisit | Practice | New evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Useful work or workload shape is undefined | Lessons 1–2 | EX-01–EX-02 | Workload and sensitivity addendum |
| Queue arithmetic or units are wrong | Lesson 3 | EX-03 | Recalculated queue worksheet |
| Measurement hides offered load | Lesson 4 | EX-04–EX-05 | New open-loop comparison |
| Fan-out tail claim is unsupported | Lesson 5 | EX-06 | Reproducible tail derivation |
| Queue/admission policy is unbounded | Lesson 6 | EX-07–EX-08 | Bound and overload trial |
| Retry/failover work is understated | Lesson 7 | EX-09–EX-10 | Amplification and reserve addendum |
| Decision lacks sweep or reversal | Lesson 8 | EX-11–EX-12 | New sweep and recorded defense |

The evaluator recommends these tasks without supplying replacement capacity
numbers or a canonical design.

## PESD 2.0 remediation

When a cross-cutting floor is missed, return to Lesson 8's PESD 2.0 extension
and the final exercise. Create a separate dated revision containing the missing
requirement/control/evidence mapping, owner, evidence boundary, failure check,
cost consequence, migration, and reversal trigger. Never edit the frozen
baseline or raw trial. A Pass creates no required remediation artifact.
