# Module 4 Remediation Map

Remediation creates a new dated artifact. Never rewrite the frozen plan, blind
diagnoses, raw telemetry, or submitted review.

| Finding | Revisit | Practice | New evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unbounded question or changed work | Lesson 1–2 | EX-01–EX-04 | New scoped rerun referencing the original |
| Broken trace/context correlation | Lesson 3 | EX-05–EX-06 | Context matrix and separate trace bundle |
| Cardinality, privacy, or overhead gap | Lesson 4 | EX-07–EX-08 | Bounded label/cost/redaction comparison |
| Weak profile or query-plan claim | Lesson 5–6 | EX-09–EX-11 | Discriminating profile/dependency rerun |
| Diagnosis inspected before freeze | Lesson 6 | EX-12 | New opaque fixture; original remains invalid |
| Flaky or unsupported regression gate | Lesson 7 | EX-13 | Raw pass/regression/inconclusive gate fixtures |
| Weak decision or teach-back | Lesson 8 | EX-14–EX-15 | Dated addendum and new recorded challenge |

An evaluator may point to these tasks. It must not supply the replacement causal
model, diagnosis, or graded decision.

## PESD 2.0 remediation

When a cross-cutting floor is missed, return to Lesson 8's PESD 2.0 extension
and the final exercise. Create a separate dated revision containing the missing
requirement/control/evidence mapping, owner, evidence boundary, failure check,
cost consequence, migration, and reversal trigger. Never edit the frozen
baseline or raw trial. A Pass creates no required remediation artifact.
