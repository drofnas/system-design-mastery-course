# Module 11 Assessment Contract

Assessment uses only immutable submitted evidence and this published contract.
Northstar fixtures calibrate the evaluator and are not commerce answers.

## Required submission

Provide one manifest resolving A01–A09 to commits or hashes: frozen Week 41
model/predictions, publication build/tests, internals review, eighteen raw paired
trials, failure matrix/investigation, asynchronous-workflow RFC, defense/dissent,
evaluation/remediation, learning logs, and assistance disclosure.

## Structural gates

### G01: Identity, completeness, provenance, and disclosure

Every artifact resolves and identifies learner, environment, implementation,
source commit, scenario/trial hashes, evidence kind, and assistance.

### G02: Preserved model and predictions

Authority, delivery, identity, ordering, partition, backlog, time, workflow, and
F01–F09 predictions predate results. Revisions are separate. Failure is hard.

### G03: Executable build and interface contract

The build exposes atomic authority/outbox, stable envelopes, publisher/log,
inbox/projection, effect keys, workflow state, poison, time, and reconciliation.
Tests and scenario/trial schemas resolve. Material absence is hard.

### G04: Immutable failure evidence and arithmetic

All pairs share inputs/seeds, change one control, and preserve raw trials.
Identities, offsets, effects, versions, attempts, lag/drain, workflow states,
watermarks, diffs, hashes, and I01–I12 recalculate. Failure is hard.

### G05: Asynchronous safety and recovery

Every repaired pair prevents lost committed publication, duplicate irreversible
effect, projection regression, poison blockage, unbounded recovery, invalid
workflow/compensation, undeclared late-data corruption, and unrepaired drift.
Failure is hard.

### G06: RFC, defense, and remediation integrity

The RFC covers alternatives, semantics, capacity, replay, reconciliation,
workflow, security, cost, migration, rollback, telemetry, owners, dissent,
uncertainty, and reversal. Remediation never overwrites evidence. Module 11
creates no Gate 4 or capstone revision.

## Scoring and result

Score R01–R10 as integers 0–4. Pass requires all gates, average at least 3.0,
no zero in R04, R06, or R09, and confidence above low. Revise covers remediable
gaps. Repeat applies when G02–G05 fails or a safety-critical criterion is zero.

## Evidence rules

- Cite `path#heading: description` for every gate and rubric row.
- Classify findings as `missing_evidence`, `incorrect_reasoning`,
  `unsupported_claim`, `invariant_failure`, `internal_contradiction`, or
  `communication_gap`.
- Do not infer production durability, availability, performance, regional
  survival, universal exactly-once effects, or security enforcement from the lab.
- Accept defensible alternatives; never require Northstar's topology or thresholds.
- Recommend named lessons/exercises without writing replacement graded work.

## Completion attestation

A frozen self-evaluated Pass establishes **Solo Complete** and remains
self-attested. A passing independent human or LLM review of the same immutable
bundle establishes **Independently Validated**.
