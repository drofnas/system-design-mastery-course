The authoritative records are in `module.json`. Every required source is free,
was reachable on 2026-08-03, has a bounded assignment, and has a complete local
lesson alternative. External work reinforces the lesson; it never replaces it.

The sources describe practices in particular organizations or ecosystems. The
learner must restate the mechanism, limits, failure model, and evidence needed
in their own environment. A source does not make a provider, service boundary,
organizational design, cost allocation, or migration technique mandatory.

Optional enrichment may include paid books such as *Team Topologies* or
*Building Evolutionary Architectures*. They are not required and no assessment
depends on access to them.
