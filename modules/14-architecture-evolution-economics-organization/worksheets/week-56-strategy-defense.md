# Week 56 Strategy and Defense Worksheet

## Strategy memo

Use the technical-strategy template. Include current evidence, alternatives,
four or more increments, dependencies, staffing, fully loaded cost, unit cost,
compatibility, security, migration gates, stopping conditions, reversals,
decommissioning, and review cadence.

## Defense protocol

Record a 20–30 minute solo defense using the module's deterministic frozen
five-question packet. A live engineer outside the chosen stack may participate
as optional stronger evidence. The learner's opening takes no more than ten
minutes. The packet challenges:

- why a boundary is needed and why the no-migration option loses;
- authority during mixed versions, backfill, cutover, and rollback;
- cost allocation, sensitivity, and budget stop;
- managed/open/custom and dependency exit;
- security and operational ownership;
- loss of the primary team;
- evidence that reverses the direction.

AI may not answer during the defense.
