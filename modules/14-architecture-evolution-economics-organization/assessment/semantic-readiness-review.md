# Module 14 Semantic Readiness Review

## Curriculum alignment

The package covers every Module 14 syllabus outcome: evidence-driven boundary
selection, social architecture, sourcing, total cost and unit economics,
compatibility, incremental migration, cutover and decommissioning, and
multi-quarter technical strategy. It contributes the required cost model,
migration plan, substantial strategy memo, failure matrix, and teach-back.

## Teaching sufficiency

Eight local lessons teach mechanisms and repeatable procedures before EX-01–18
and the independent worksheets require them. Every lesson includes outcomes,
prerequisites, explanation, a derivation or decision procedure, the separate
Northstar example, expert mistakes, guided practice, self-checks with explained
answers, and authoritative citations. External sources reinforce rather than
replace local instruction.

## Case and capstone isolation

Northstar's operations registry remains authoritative for observations and
publication approval; only its derived public catalog projection and bulletin
delivery capability evolve. No commerce entity, topology, staffing decision,
threshold, or canonical capstone answer is supplied. Learners freeze independent
commerce work before opening the case or answer key. Existing baselines and Gate
revisions are untouched; corrections require separate dated artifacts.

## Interface and evidence integrity

The public schemas constrain exact scenario and trial contracts. Eighteen
deterministic scenarios form nine same-input broken/repaired pairs; each changes
one named control, preserves hashes, exposes its target failed invariant, and
restores I01–I12 after repair. The model reports compatibility, migration state,
write authority, shadow comparison, cutover, rollback, cost, dependency, and
ownership evidence without claiming production proof.

## Operational and organizational judgment

Instruction and assessment connect migration decisions to authority, security,
operations, recovery, cost allocation, dependency limits, ownership, cognitive
load, succession, staffing, dissent, governance expiry, stops, reversals, and
decommissioning. SemVer is presented as one public-contract convention, not a
universal compatibility proof.

## Assessment integrity

G01–G06 run before R01–R10. R05–R09 are safety-critical. Evaluators cite only
submitted files and headings, classify findings, accept defensible alternatives,
and recommend lessons and exercises without supplying replacement graded work.
Pass, Revise, and Repeat were each evaluated twice in isolated invocations;
bands agree and maximum criterion drift is one.

## Conclusion

Semantic review passes. No missing teaching dependency, capstone answer leakage,
learner-evidence overwrite, unsupported proof, fabricated authority, unsafe
operator instruction, or Phase IV scope drift was found. Module 15 retains Gate
5 and runtime comparisons.
