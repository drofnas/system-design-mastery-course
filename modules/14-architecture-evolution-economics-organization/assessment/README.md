# Module 14 Assessment Contract

Evaluate only submitted evidence against the published rubric. Run structural
gates before semantic scoring. Cite `path#heading` for every gate, score, and
finding. Do not infer hidden migration controls or reward architecture labels.

## Structural gates

### G01: Submission identity and completeness

A01–A11, commit/hash identity, assistance disclosure, and reachable evidence are
present. Missing required artifacts prevents Pass.

### G02: Frozen chronology and evidence integrity (hard gate)

Week 76 baseline and F01–F09 predictions predate trials. Raw broken results
remain unchanged; repairs and interpretation are separate dated evidence. A
changed or fabricated first attempt yields Repeat.

### G03: Paired lab contract (hard gate)

F01–F09 contain exactly one broken and repaired trial with the same shared-input
hash, one named control change, schema-valid output, a failed target invariant,
and I01–I12 passing after repair.

### G04: Compatibility, authority, data, and cutover safety (hard gate)

Mixed versions, schema phases, backfill, write authority, reconciliation,
shadow comparison, promotion, and rollback have deterministic evidence. An
unresolved incompatible effect, ambiguous authority, data loss, cross-scope
disclosure, or lossy claimed rollback yields Repeat.

### G05: Economic, dependency, and ownership control (hard gate)

Fully loaded cost, good-outcome denominator, threshold, dependency exit, primary
and secondary ownership, access, runbook, and handoff exercise are present. A
known unbounded cost, critical dependency with no containment, or unowned
critical capability yields Repeat.

### G06: Strategy, defense, and remediation

The strategy compares alternatives, sequences evidence-gated increments, names
staffing and dependencies, records dissent, and defines stop, reversal, and
decommission conditions. The defense includes follow-up questions without AI;
remediation creates a separate artifact and points to published lessons.

## Scoring and result

Score R01–R10 as integers 0–4. Pass requires every gate, every required artifact,
an average of at least 3.0, and no zero in R05–R09. Repeat applies when G02–G05
fails or a safety-critical criterion is zero. Other incomplete but repairable
work is Revise.

Distinguish `missing_evidence`, `incorrect_reasoning`, `unsupported_claim`,
`invariant_failure`, `internal_contradiction`, and `communication_gap`. A
defensible modular, service, event, managed, open, custom, or no-migration choice
is not a defect because it differs from Northstar.

## Evidence boundary

The lab cannot prove production compatibility, migration safety at scale,
provider portability, accounting accuracy, legal or security compliance, real
human staffing resilience, or multi-quarter organizational results.

## Completion attestation

A frozen self-evaluated Pass establishes **Solo Complete** and remains
self-attested. A passing independent human or LLM review of the same immutable
bundle establishes **Independently Validated**.
