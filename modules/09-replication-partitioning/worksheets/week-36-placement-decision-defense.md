# Week 36 Placement Decision and Defense

1. Map every important operation to consistency, acknowledgement, partition, and repair behavior.
2. Compare leader/follower, multi-leader, and leaderless alternatives under shared drivers.
3. State partition key, hotspot controls, regional/residency constraints, and measured capacity/cost.
4. Plan compatibility, copy/verify, cutover, rollback, reconciliation, and decommissioning.
5. Name application, data, security, platform, finance, and on-call owners.
6. Record dissent, uncertainty, reversal thresholds, and expired-exception cleanup.
7. Defend without changing the workload or failure model; preserve questions and answers.
8. Complete Module 9 evaluation and Gate 3. Remediate only in dated addenda.
