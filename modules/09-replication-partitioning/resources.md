# Module 9 Resource Guide

The manifest is the machine-readable resource record. All seven required items
are free, bounded, and paired with a local lesson that teaches the mechanism
without the link. Verify the URLs and access notes again when beginning study.

For each resource, submit the bounded assignment named in `module.json`. Record
the accessed date, sections used, one claim supported, one limitation, and one
question the source does not settle. The USENIX DynamoDB paper is the required
written source; its presentation is optional media. No required outcome depends
on video captions.

Do not treat a paper's workload, implementation, or failure model as a universal
recommendation. Cite the source when using its observations, and label any
transfer to the learner's environment as a hypothesis until tested.
