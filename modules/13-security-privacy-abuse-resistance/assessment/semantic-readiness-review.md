# Module 13 Semantic Readiness Review

## Curriculum alignment

The package covers every Module 13 syllabus question and required topic. It
connects threat modeling to identity and session lifecycles, per-object/action
authorization, tenant isolation, secret/key custody, audit, privacy and data
lifecycle, dependency provenance, abuse economics, prompt injection,
deterministic tool authorization, response, and security architecture.

## Teaching sufficiency

Eight local lessons teach mechanisms and repeatable decision procedures before
EX-01–EX-18 and the independent worksheets require them. Every lesson contains
outcomes, prerequisites, explanation, a derivation or procedure, a worked
Northstar example, expert mistakes, guided practice, self-checks, explained
answers, and authoritative citations. Required sources reinforce rather than
replace local instruction.

## Case and capstone isolation

Northstar continues the observatory case with research tenants, private
observations, publication, operators, retained copies, dependencies, budgets,
and retrieved operating notes. It introduces no commerce entities or canonical
capstone topology. Teaching, answer keys, and fixtures require the learner to
freeze independent commerce work first. The Week 1 baseline and existing Gate
revisions are not modified; Module 13 corrections use new dated artifacts.

## Interface and evidence integrity

The public schemas constrain exact top-level scenario and trial contracts.
Eighteen deterministic scenarios form nine same-input broken/repaired pairs;
each pair changes one named control, preserves shared-input hashes, exposes its
declared broken invariant, and restores I01–I12 when repaired. Scenario,
shared-input, and configuration hashes preserve the comparison boundary.

## Operational judgment

Instruction and assessment cover security, privacy, operations, cost, tenant
fairness, ownership, staffing, migration, rollout, recovery, dissent,
communication, audit, and reversal evidence. Laws and compliance obligations
remain environment-specific inputs owned by qualified stakeholders.

## Assessment integrity

G01–G06 run before R01–R10. R02, R03, R04, R05, R07, and R09 are
safety-critical. Evaluators cite submitted files and headings, distinguish
finding classes, accept defensible alternatives, and recommend lessons and
exercises without writing replacement graded work. Pass, Revise, and Repeat
fixtures were each evaluated twice; bands and every criterion agree exactly.

## Evidence boundary

The deterministic lab models decisions and evidence. It does not prove
production isolation, cryptographic strength, physical deletion, real
supply-chain integrity, legal compliance, or adaptive-adversary resistance.

## Conclusion

Semantic review passes. No missing teaching dependency, capstone answer
leakage, learner-evidence overwrite, unsupported proof, unsafe operator
