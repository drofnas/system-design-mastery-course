# Week 12 Systems Report and Defense

## Report contract

1. Scope, decision, and frozen prediction
2. Environment and equivalent-work proof
3. Raw observation table with repetitions and uncertainty
4. Counterintuitive result
5. Causal chain and two competing explanations
6. Falsification experiments
7. Invariant, security, durability, and recovery effects
8. Production-transfer comparison
9. Decision, alternatives, cost, owners, rollout, rollback, reversal
10. Limitations and next evidence

## Defense

Record 12–15 minutes. Answer without changing submitted workload or failure model:

- Which result disproved your model?
- Which counter is only a symptom?
- What does your durability result not prove?
- Who owns resource limits and incident response?
- What production difference would block transfer?
- Which evidence reverses the decision?

## Revision rule

Keep prediction, raw evidence, and first report unchanged. Create a separately
named revision that cites the evaluator finding, lesson, exercise, and new evidence.
