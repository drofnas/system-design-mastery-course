# Module 1 Remediation Map

Remediation is a new dated artifact. Never rewrite the Week 1 baseline or the
submission that was scored.

| Finding | Revisit | Practice | New evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outcome, scope, or workload is vague | Lessons 1–2 | EX-01–EX-03 | Bounded problem-frame addendum |
| Invariant or authority is unsupported | Lesson 3 | EX-04–EX-05 | State/authority proof with falsifier |
| Quality scenario is not measurable | Lesson 4 | EX-06 | Six-part scenario correction |
| Context boundary hides ownership | Lesson 5 | EX-07 | Corrected context and trust-boundary note |
| Candidate or reversibility analysis is weak | Lesson 6 | EX-08–EX-09 | Separate decision addendum |
| Failure claim lacks a mechanism | Lesson 7 | EX-10–EX-11 | New failure matrix and test |
| Defense rewards labels over causality | Lesson 8 | EX-12 | New recorded challenge response |

An evaluator may point to these tasks but must not author the learner's graded
architecture or overwrite the preserved baseline.
