# Week 31: Failure and Restore Matrix

For F01–F07 record prediction commit, scenario/config hash, environment, raw
trial path/hash, authoritative invariant, broken observation, at least one
alternative cause, isolated repair, same-input proof, rerun, and uncertainty.

For recovery include backup identity, base checkpoint/LSN, required WAL range,
target, archive completeness, checksum results, committed/loser transactions,
redo/undo evidence, acknowledged/durable set, derived rebuild, traffic gate,
RTO, RPO, workload size, and owner. Preserve raw evidence and the first restore
report; remediation belongs in a new artifact.
