# Week 32: Transaction and Recovery Decision

Use shared drivers to compare at least three concurrency/constraint strategies
and two backup/recovery strategies. Map every invariant to its authority,
boundary, constraint, isolation, retry rule, durable-ack rule, backup method,
tested target, telemetry, owner, and reversal evidence.

Include security of backups and restore credentials, retention and deletion,
archive/storage/abort/restore cost, capacity during recovery, mixed-version
migration, rollback, compatibility, decommissioning, and exception handling.
Record database, application, security, finance, and on-call challenge
questions; dissent; changed belief; unresolved risk; and teach-back evidence.
