# Module 8 Resource Guide

The manifest is the machine-readable source for authors, URLs, bounded
assignments, time, access, verification date, purpose, and local fallback.
Required resources are free. External material reinforces rather than replaces
Lessons 1–8.

## Reading order and evidence

| Week | Resources | Evidence to retain |
|---:|---|---|
| 29 | RES-01 | Annotated histories, anomaly-to-invariant map, vendor-specific caveat |
| 30 | RES-02–RES-03 | Wait-for graph, visibility timeline, full-transaction retry boundary |
| 31 | RES-04–RES-06 | Flush-order diagram, durability assumptions, recoverable-set and RPO calculation |
| 32 | RES-07–RES-08 | Control/owner analysis, recovery decision, unresolved operating risk |

For each resource record one claim, its exact boundary, one assumption, one
Northstar application, one independent commerce question, and the evidence
that would falsify the claim. If a video or external page is unavailable, use
the manifest's local lesson alternative and complete the same evidence task.

Do not copy article or video text. Cite short necessary quotations, otherwise
write original explanations and preserve the source URL and verification date.
