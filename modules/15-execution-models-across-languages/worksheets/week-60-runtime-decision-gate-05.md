# Week 60 Runtime Decision, Defense, and Gate 5

## Comparison reports

Complete one workload-behavior comparison and one memory/scheduler/type internals
comparison. Cite frozen predictions and raw evidence. Separate conformance from
performance and host observations from language guarantees.

## ADR

Compare keep-current, bounded adoption, and broad adoption. Include user/business
outcomes, workload, safety, operability, ecosystem, security, cost, ownership,
migration, rollback, dissent, stopping conditions, and reversal evidence.

## Defense

Present for ten minutes as a recording or transcript and answer the frozen
solo-review packet from security, platform, finance, on-call, and team-ownership
perspectives without live AI. Record changed claims and follow-ups. A live panel
is optional.

## Assessment and remediation

Freeze the module submission, run the evaluator, preserve its output, and create
a separate remediation revision. Then complete [Gate 5](../assessment/gate-05.md).
Do not create or edit a Week 60 capstone revision.
