# Module 15 Resource Guide

The manifest records publisher, URL, type, access, week, time, purpose, exact
boundary, verification date, and local alternative. All required resources are
free. Use the local lesson when a site or caption track is unavailable.

## Week 57

- **Node.js event loop:** produce a scheduler-placement table, not an advocacy note.
- **Go memory model and race detector:** RES-02 and RES-09 produce a
  happens-before graph and one detector limitation. A clean detector run is
  evidence, not proof.
- **Rust ownership and Send/Sync:** RES-03 and RES-10 annotate transfer, borrow,
  release, and compile-time rejection points.
- **Java virtual threads:** distinguish task abundance from admitted work.
- **TypeScript compatibility:** show why compile-time shape cannot validate JSON.
- **Go scheduler video:** watch with captions or use Lesson 2; identify a claim
  that requires verification against the pinned runtime.
- **Toolchain lifecycle sources:** optional RES-12–RES-15 support provenance and
  lifecycle review; the pinned lockfile and local lessons are sufficient for the lab.
