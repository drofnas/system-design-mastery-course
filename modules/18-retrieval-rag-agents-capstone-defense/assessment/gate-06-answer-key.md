# Gate 6 assessor reasoning guide

This guide defines boundaries, not a canonical commerce architecture.

- Written calculations must state their denominator, judgment convention, units, and decision consequence. Correct arithmetic without a causal interpretation is incomplete.
- The hidden CivicAid practical should identify the earliest failed trust or durability boundary, not merely the visible bad answer. Accept alternative repairs that restore every invariant under the same workload.
- The commerce defense must stand on independent evidence. Do not reward copying CivicAid components or penalize a different defensible design.
- Portfolio review should show preserved chronology and changed judgment. A polished final artifact cannot compensate for edited history.
- Reasonable uncertainty is acceptable when bounded by harm, a measurable next experiment, and an owner. Fabricated certainty is not.

Assessors must not disclose hidden practical inputs or draft replacement learner answers in feedback.
