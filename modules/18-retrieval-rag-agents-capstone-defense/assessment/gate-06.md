# Gate 6 — Final capstone defense

Submit at `reviews/gate-06-submission.md`. Assessors preserve hidden prompts and do not publish a canonical commerce answer.

## Part A: Written examination — 60 minutes

Answer bounded questions that require retrieval metric calculations, exact/ANN trade-offs, evidence/version reasoning, tool authorization, replay/idempotency, cancellation/budget behavior, and cross-module capacity/security/operations consequences. Show assumptions and units.

## Part B: Hidden CivicAid practical — 90 minutes

Diagnose a previously unseen combination spanning Modules 16–18. Produce a causal timeline, failed invariant, minimal safe repair, validation test, operating/cost consequence, residual uncertainty, and owner. The workload and expected invariant are fixed by the assessor.

## Part C: Independent commerce architecture defense — 30 minutes

Defend the frozen AI Shopping Assistant across product, technical, security, cost, ownership, migration, reversal, and operating challenges. Cite submission evidence. The assessor evaluates reasoning, not similarity to CivicAid.

## Part D: Portfolio review — 30 minutes

Trace at least three decisions from early baseline to final revision, including one reversed decision, one failure-driven change, and one cross-team ownership decision. Cite the preserved Week 1, 12, 24, 48, and 72 artifacts.

## Assessor review record

Record technical, product, security, cost, ownership, and operating review separately with `path#heading` evidence. State uncertainty boundaries and follow-up questions without drafting the learner's answer.

## Result

Gate 6/final capstone Pass requires all six course gates, average ≥3.5, I01–I12 passing, and all six review dimensions successful. Otherwise record Revise or Repeat under the assessment contract.
