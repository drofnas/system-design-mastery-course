# Performance Review

## Submission identity and scope

Record author, date, commits, workload, user journey, useful-work definition,
environment, and tool/AI disclosure.

## Baseline and question

Cite the frozen baseline and investigation question. State exclusions and the
smallest meaningful effect.

## Observations

Cite raw trace, metric, log, profile, query-plan, and benchmark evidence. Keep
observations separate from interpretation.

## Causal model and alternatives

Explain the mechanism connecting code, resource behavior, and user outcome.
List credible alternatives, falsifiers, and the discriminating experiment.

## Proposed change and validation

Describe equivalent-work checks, candidate change, interleaved repetitions,
effect and uncertainty, telemetry overhead, and unresolved limitations.

## Decision and regression budget

State accept/reject/continue-investigation, the enforced budget, and the action
when it is exceeded.

## Operations, security, cost, and ownership

Cover rollout, rollback, monitoring, cardinality, redaction, access, retention,
collection cost, service owner, observability owner, and incident use.

## Reversal and follow-up

Name production evidence that reverses the decision, follow-up experiments,
owners, and due dates.
