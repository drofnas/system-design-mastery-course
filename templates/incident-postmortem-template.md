## Mitigation and recovery

Explain why each mitigation was safe, what it changed, and how impact moved.
Keep mitigation distinct from later causal diagnosis.

## Trigger, contributing conditions, and causal evidence

Separate the initiating event from conditions that expanded impact. Include
discriminating evidence, falsified alternatives, and remaining uncertainty.
